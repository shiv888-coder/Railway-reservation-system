👴 Senior Citizen Concession (40%)

Travelers aged 60 and above automatically receive a 40% discount on base fare across eligible express and superfast classes.

🆔 Mandatory Government ID

Every ticketed passenger must specify a valid identification: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License.

👥 Maximum 6 Passengers / Booking

Under Indian Railways reservation guidelines, a maximum of 6 travelers can be accommodated on a single master PNR reservation slip.
